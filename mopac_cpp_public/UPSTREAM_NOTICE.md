# Upstream provenance and license notice

MOPAC-CPP is a C++17 translation of the MOPAC2016 numerical semantics.

- The translation is based on the open-source MOPAC source, archived at Zenodo
  (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6511958). Open-source MOPAC is numerically
  identical to MOPAC2016, as stated in the official FAQ
  (https://openmopac.net/Manual/trouble_shooting.html).
